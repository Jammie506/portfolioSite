Privacy & Cookies

This site only uses a small first-party cookie to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode).

Cookie Details

  • Cookie name: preferred-theme
  • Purpose: Stores your chosen theme so pages stay consistent while you browse.
  • Type: First-party functional cookie
  • Duration: 1 year
  • Shared with third parties: No

How to Change It

You can change your preference any time using the Dark mode toggle visible on each page. If you clear site data in your browser, this preference is removed and the site returns to your device default theme setting.
